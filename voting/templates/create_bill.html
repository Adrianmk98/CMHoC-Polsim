Bill Submission Guidelines
Bill Numbers:
  • C-###: Bills originating in the House of Commons
  • S-###: Bills originating in the Senate
Bill Statuses:
  • Draft: Bill is being prepared
  • First Reading: Bill introduced for first time
  • Second Reading: Main debate and vote
  • Committee: Under committee review
  • Third Reading: Final debate and vote
  • Royal Assent: Bill has passed and become law
Writing Tips:
  • Be clear and specific about what the bill does
  • Organize the full text with numbered sections
  • Include definitions if needed
  • Specify when the bill would come into force
